NetSpeed Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

NetSpeed (package com.jdevio.netspeed) is an internet speed test and network monitor. It requires no account and no login. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.
  • Test history and preferences stay on your device.
  • Speed tests send anonymous sample traffic to Cloudflare and an OVH mirror fallback.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising ID.
  • Location permission is used only to read Wi-Fi names and scan nearby networks.

What is stored on your device

DataPurposeLocation
Speed test resultsHistory, grading, sharingLocal app storage
PreferencesUnit, theme, widget and monitor settingsLocal app storage
Last widget valuesWidget displayLocal preferences

Delete everything from History → Clear history or by uninstalling the app.

Network requests

  • Cloudflare speed endpoints — download/upload sample traffic and a zero-byte latency request. Cloudflare receives your IP address and normal request metadata.
  • Cloudflare trace — returns your public IP, country and edge location for the test label and IP information screen.
  • OVH mirror — fallback download source when Cloudflare rate-limits a test.
  • Public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers (Cloudflare, Google, Quad9) — only when you run the DNS lookup tool.
  • Google AdMob — banner and optional rewarded ads, subject to Google's policies.

No diagnostics, browsing history, contacts, files or app usage are uploaded.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — run tests and identify the connection.
  • Wi-Fi state / nearby Wi-Fi devices — Wi-Fi details and nearby scan.
  • Fine location — Android requires it to read the current SSID, BSSID and scan results. GPS is never used and location is never transmitted.
  • Notifications — show the live speed notification.
  • Foreground service — keep the status-bar monitor running (Android requires an ongoing notification).
  • Boot completed — optionally resume the monitor after a restart.

Advertising

The app shows one banner on the Speed Test screen and offers an optional rewarded ad that hides the banner for 24 hours. Google AdMob may process your advertising ID as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

NetSpeed is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All requests use HTTPS with the system trust store. Cleartext traffic is disabled.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date is updated and the new policy ships with the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email work@jdevio.com. We usually reply within two business days.
